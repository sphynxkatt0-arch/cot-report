Data integrity & methodology

Every score stays tied to the underlying weekly report, governed model version and source taxonomy.

Index futures

S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, VIX, Russell 2000 and Dow use CFTC Traders in Financial Futures where available, with Legacy retained as an alternate view.

Gold & Silver

Physical commodities use CFTC Disaggregated Futures Only. Under model v1.2, Managed Money is the sole directional metals input; Producer/Merchant, Swap Dealers, Other Reportables and Non-reportables remain visible as context.

Model governance

Dealer/Intermediary, Legacy Commercial and Total Reportable carry zero directional weight. Any other secondary actor must demonstrate incremental lookahead-safe holdout value before receiving a non-zero production weight.

Release timing

COT observations are Tuesday “as of” positions released later in the week. The dashboard displays the report date explicitly and warns when an expected refresh is stale.
