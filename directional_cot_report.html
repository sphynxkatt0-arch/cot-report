How to read it

Positive means Treasury withdrawals put cash into the private sector. Negative means deposits—especially tax receipts—pulled cash into the TGA. A rising TGA is therefore treated as a drain. This panel explains the liquidity path and may reduce risk, but it cannot reverse the COT structural direction.
